## Primary audiences

- Newcomers seeking to translate international experience into the Canadian workplace
- Career changers moving into new professional areas
- Underemployed professionals seeking better-aligned opportunities
- Employers seeking training, recruitment support and talent pipelines
- Community partners supporting workforce development

## Core service pillars

### Career development and employment support

Career assessment, career planning, resume and interview support, job-search preparation and employment pathways.

### Upskilling and professional training

Practical training in project management, business analysis, data analysis, AI and automation, and related workplace skills.

### Certifications

Preparation pathways for PMP, CAPM, Scrum Master, Six Sigma and Lean Six Sigma certifications.

### Employer and workforce solutions

Customized training, recruitment support, talent pipelines and work-placement partnerships.

## Homepage structure

| Order | Section | Primary purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hero | State the value proposition and present the main CTA |
| 2 | Audience cards | Help visitors identify the most relevant pathway |
| 3 | Career journey | Explain the process from assessment to employment/project delivery |
| 4 | Service pillars | Summarize the four main areas of support |
| 5 | Featured programs | Surface priority training and certifications |
| 6 | Success stories | Provide credible social proof |
| 7 | Employer/community | Present partnership opportunities |
| 8 | EPIC values | Explain the organization’s operating values |
| 9 | Final CTA | Direct visitors to assessment, consultation or partnership |
| 10 | Footer | Provide secondary navigation, newsletter and legal links |

## Content rules

- Use direct, benefit-led headings.
- Avoid unverified claims, statistics or employment guarantees.
- Confirm course dates, prices and delivery methods before publication.
- Keep one clear primary CTA per section.
- Use consistent names for programs across navigation, pages and forms.
- Add descriptive alternative text to meaningful images.
